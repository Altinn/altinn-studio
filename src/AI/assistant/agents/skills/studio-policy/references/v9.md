## In v9 apps

The v9 workflow engine moves each instance through the process as the service owner, not as the end user. The policy must therefore give the service owner these rights:

- `read` and `write`, in every process step
- `complete`, at the end of the process
- `delete`, if the app deletes its instances when the process ends
- `confirm`, if the process has a `confirmation` task

A new v9 app already has this rule. Its subject matches `urn:altinn:org` in the `access-subject` category against `[org]`. Keep it when you remove rights from other roles.
